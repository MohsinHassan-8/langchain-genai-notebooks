The Journey of Dawn

When night had wrapped the world in shade,
And silent dreams through darkness strayed,
A lonely traveler walked afar,
Guided only by a distant star.

The roads were rough, the winds were cold,
The path ahead unknown, untold.
Yet in his heart there burned a flame,
A quiet hope without a name.

He crossed the valleys deep and wide,
Where restless rivers rushed with pride.
He climbed the mountains crowned with snow,
Through storms that raged and fierce winds blew.

At times he stumbled, weak and worn,
His spirit tired, his courage torn.
The shadows whispered, "Turn away,
No light awaits beyond this day."

But still he rose with steady grace,
Determination on his face.
For every night, however long,
Must yield at last to morning's song.

Along the way he met a few—
Kind souls whose friendship quickly grew.
They shared their stories, hopes, and fears,
Their laughter easing heavy years.

Together they would walk awhile,
Transforming hardship with a smile.
Though some departed, paths apart,
They left their footprints on his heart.

Seasons changed as time moved on;
Green summers faded into dawns
Of autumn leaves in golden flight,
Then winters clothed the earth in white.

And through it all he came to see
That life's true worth could never be
Measured only by the end,
But by each moment, foe, and friend.

One morning, standing on a hill,
The world around him calm and still,
He watched the sun begin to rise,
Painting gold across the skies.

Then softly, as the daylight grew,
He understood what once he never knew:
The journey was the treasure sought,
The lesson every mile had taught.

For dreams are not just distant lands,
But courage held in trembling hands;
And hope is not a fleeting spark,
But light carried through the dark.

So when your road seems steep and long,
And doubt grows louder than your song,
Remember dawn awaits the night—
Keep walking on toward the light.
